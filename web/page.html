Rules

A coin decides whether the asset is worth 900 or 1100, and you never see it. Quote a price you would buy at and a price you would sell at. Eight traders then come along, one at a time, and each trades once with you. Anyone who knows the value buys if it's 1100 and sells if it's 900. Everyone else flips a coin.

Before each new quote, say how likely 1100 now seems. Your first quote comes before any trade, when the chance is 50%. Aim for the tightest quotes that wouldn't lose you money on average. No calculator, no notes: the point is to record your own judgement.
